# Ar pin-to-pin discharge

This case simulates an Ar discharge at 100 Torr between two paraboloid shaped 
electrodes separated by 4 mm and powered by a 1 kV anode (bottom pin). 
The simulations indicate anode and cathode directed streamers and their bridging
over ~ 20 ns. This case also can compare the use of a sharp-interface-scheme for the 
boundaries versus a stair-step. `prob.sharp_interface_scheme=1` enables the more accurate
non-conformal boundary resolving scheme.
